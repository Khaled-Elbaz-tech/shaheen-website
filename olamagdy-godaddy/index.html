A practice shaped by two countries, different disciplines, and the movement between buildings, landscapes and cities.

Practice across
Egypt & India

Ola Magdy is an architect, designer and educator whose career spans professional practice in Egypt and India. Since 2016, she has progressed from a junior architectural role to senior practice, university teaching and her current position as Principal Associate Designer at Archohm.

Her early career in Egypt included Bonyan Designs, Ebtekarak and Eiffel. In India, her experience expanded through a Visiting Professor appointment at the World University of Design, followed by Senior Architect roles at Srishti Architects and Creative Group. She joined Archohm in 2024.

At Archohm, her portfolio extends from cultural and civic buildings to healthcare, housing, hospitality and public landscapes. The work presented here includes proposals across Meghalaya, Bihar and Bodoland, where individual buildings sit within wider questions of terrain, movement, community and urban growth.

This range is central to her practice: moving between the scale of a room, the organisation of a campus and the structure of a city. Her experience across design studios and architectural education brings both the development of ideas and the communication of design into her work.

A way of working

From observation
to architecture.

Physical models, drawings and visual studies help Ola explore how an idea becomes a place. Site visits and conversations with project stakeholders bring the realities of landscape, use and context into that process.

Her interest in architecture extends to experiencing buildings firsthand: observing how daylight enters a room, how materials meet and how people move through a space. That attention connects the larger planning studies in this portfolio with the more intimate experience of a building.
