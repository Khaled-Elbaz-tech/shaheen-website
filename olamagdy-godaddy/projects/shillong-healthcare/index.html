Shillong Healthcare Campuses

Explore the project

The idea / Shillong, Meghalaya

A group of proposals for Civil Hospital, Red Cross accommodation and Ganesh Das Hospital. The work covers campus reorganisation, clinical blocks, staff housing and the relationship between hospital buildings and restorative outdoor spaces.
