Shillong Public Realm

Explore the project

The idea / Shillong, Meghalaya

A connected set of proposals for the Polo riverfront, Ward’s Lake and surrounding public spaces. The work combines landscape, movement, recreation and supporting buildings, with design development at both city and building scale.

Polo riverfront masterplan

Sports grounds and new public spaces share a continuous river edge.

02

Riverfront connections

The promenade combines planted edges, gathering spaces and routes between destinations.

03

Ward’s Lake Promenade

The wider public-space network connects the lake to cafés, gardens and the riverfront.

04

Ward’s Lake Redevelopment

The proposals bring new facilities into the existing landscape through a series of focused interventions.

05

Ward’s Lake Experience Centre

A vertical marker rises above a low building integrated with the lakeside terrain.

06

Exhibition spaces

The experience centre develops a sequence of themed interiors and open public spaces.

07

Sports hostel

Accommodation and shared functions occupy a building with a clear public frontage and internal gathering spaces.

08

Polo Multi-Level Parking & SRGT Sports Tower

The Polo parking and sports studies combine infrastructure with a compact vertical programme.

09

Polo Hawkers’ Market

Trading space and everyday facilities complete the public-realm programme.

10

Governor’s Herbal Garden

Elevated paths, polyhouses, an amphitheatre and spaces for play and wellbeing are set among existing trees near Ward’s Lake.

11

Polo Sports Towers

The riverfront masterplan studies two vertical sports programmes: an SRGT tower and a second tower on the smaller Part F site. Courts, changing facilities and public uses are stacked to work within the available footprints.
