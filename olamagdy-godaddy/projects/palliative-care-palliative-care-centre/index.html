The idea / Muzaffarpur, Bihar

A care campus organised around existing trees, courtyards and sheltered circulation. The architecture combines clinical and everyday spaces with related support buildings.

Building around the trees

Care at ground level

The section through care

Low profiles and shaded circulation maintain a close relationship between interiors and outdoor space.

The everyday experience

Daylight, views and accessible garden edges give the shared spaces a domestic scale.
